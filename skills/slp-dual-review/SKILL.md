# SLP Dual Review

Assess the same candidate against an explicit contract using two independent reviews, then resolve findings with evidence. This is a personalized SLP derivative; a separate dual-review protocol has not been established as an exact original doctrine. Metadata describes ownership, not runtime admission or authority.

## Select and bound

Use when two reviews are required, the cost of a missed defect is material, proof is weak or significant counterevidence remains unresolved. For a simple, readily verified change, use proportionate ordinary review. If the task needs competing solutions, use an admitted design workflow. If the contract is undecided, identify the decision owner before treating tests as acceptance criteria; exploratory review may inform that decision but cannot silently freeze a new contract.

Do not substitute this method for an explicitly requested Council, triple-review or another mandatory workflow. Their trigger, required methods and receipts remain in force.

Lead selects the method, coordinates Peers, checks evidence and issues the disposition within its acceptance authority. Reviewers do not accept the work or patch their own findings. Supervisor verifies attention episodes and advises Lead through the bound channel; ordinary Peer coordination stays with Lead. If Lead authored the candidate and policy requires Human acceptance, preserve that requirement even if both reviewers find no issue.

Verify Lead admission, applicable trigger, exact project, delegation lease and budget before launch. Follow the current Beads checkpoint and issue-grant policy. Use the exposed Paseo tool schema, approved routes and parent-owned Peer assignments; do not invent role identities, use native delegation as a fallback or create Council cases/receipts for ordinary dual review. Missing prerequisites block the dependent action, not unrelated authorized work. Reuse authorization already granted.

## Freeze the review packet

Send both reviewers the same core packet:

```text
Original intent and decision requested
Accepted contract and acceptance criteria with provenance
Exact base and candidate identity; complete relevant change boundary
Authorized source snapshot, dependencies and observations
Known proof: commands/results and their exact candidate, with gaps marked
Required review coverage and excluded scope
Allowed effects, resources, tracker checkpoints and issue grants
Report destination, follow-up budget and stop condition
```

For a multi-commit change, use the actual intended base; do not assume HEAD's parent describes the whole change. A mutable path or digest alone is not a preserved snapshot. Require a recoverable candidate or explicitly hold acceptance until stability can be established. For a document or design artifact, pin its exact version and applicable requirements in the same way.

Supply necessary facts, constraints and source material without the desired verdict, author self-assessment or sibling findings. Logs and tests are evidence to inspect, not endorsements to repeat. If a known incident or defect is the review target, include its factual reproduction in both packets; do not hide necessary information for artificial blindness. Report prior exposure and limit the independence claim when prior conclusions are part of the authorized input.

Choose two fresh non-author Peer reviewers with the same baseline review obligation. Additional lenses may differ, but neither reviewer should inspect only a disjoint half of the candidate. An architect proposing a design plus a reviewer examining it is not dual review. Discover and pin suitable approved profiles; provider diversity may help but is not proof of independence. Inability to obtain two qualified reviewers is a coverage gap, not permission to relabel the author.

Read [the reviewer brief](references/reviewer-brief.md) and attach its participant instructions plus the case packet to each assignment. Do not pass parent conversation history, orchestration instructions or sibling identity/report sources that reveal first-round conclusions.

## Independent first round

Each reviewer inspects the same candidate and contract independently. Keep the assignment read-only. Executing checks requires that their actual filesystem, service and external effects fit the assignment; tests can write artifacts or contact services. If a required check needs additional effects, report that gap for a separate authorized check rather than claiming it ran.

Require findings to distinguish observed failure, supported inference and untested concern. A finding needs the candidate/source location, triggering conditions, expected versus actual behavior, consequence, evidence or reproduction, and the smallest correction or disproof. Prioritize by demonstrated impact and likelihood. Do not demand a finding quota, redesign by preference or generic risk lists.

A no-findings report must identify what was inspected, checks actually run, coverage gaps and residual uncertainty. Test success, reviewer confidence and a terminal notification are not acceptance.

Wait for both reviewers' terminal notifications before reading their first-round conclusions. Inspect delivery/lifecycle trouble when necessary without feeding partial verdicts into the unfinished review. Preserve the original reports and their available evidence identifiers before any cross-exchange.

Check observable input/activity evidence for snapshot identity, author independence, unauthorized mutations and sibling exposure. Classify missing, stale or compromised coverage explicitly. Instructions supply audited procedural isolation, not a technical sandbox. If one report is unusable, the result is not a completed dual review. Any replacement or rerun must fit existing topology and budget; do not retry until approval appears.

## Adjudicate findings

Lead deduplicates by underlying failure mechanism while retaining both source references and distinct affected cases. One supported finding outweighs another reviewer's silence. Two matching claims still need evidence; different severity estimates do not determine truth by vote or averaging.

For each material finding, record one disposition with rationale: confirmed and needs correction, disproved, accepted limitation by an authorized owner, or unresolved. A speculative concern may justify a narrow check; it does not automatically mandate a broader redesign. Preserve verified decisions unless new evidence or changed requirements justify reopening them.

For a material disagreement, relay a neutral, specific question after the independent round. Bound the exchange to the assignment budget and a fact or assumption that can change the verdict. If more discussion yields no evidence, obtain an authorized discriminating check or retain the uncertainty. Do not require agreement, manufacture opposition or discard dissent to finish.

## Corrections and final disposition

Reviewers hand findings to Lead; fixes go to the authorized writer. A patch creates a new candidate. Prior reviews remain evidence for the old candidate and must not be presented as approval of the new one.

Record the correction delta and choose revalidation by impact. A bounded verification can establish that a specific finding is resolved; it does not establish two independent full reviews of the revised candidate. Reopen affected contract, dependencies and regression coverage when corrections change them. If two independent reviews are required for the final candidate, obtain both under the applicable authorization.

Issue the final packet through the existing durable handback:

```text
ACCEPT / REOPEN / REJECT / UNKNOWN within the current acceptance authority
Exact base/candidate and applicable contract
Original report references and independence/coverage evidence
Material findings and individual dispositions with evidence
Checks run, checks omitted and candidate-specific revalidation
Remaining dissent, accepted limitations and unresolved decisions
Next owner/action, reopen conditions and any Human acceptance required
```

Do not accept while mandatory proof or material contract decisions remain unresolved. Apply issue lifecycle and author/acceptor separation from the current policy. Acceptance does not grant commit, publish, deploy or cleanup authority. End the review at handback; later implementation or lifecycle actions require their proper assignment.

## Attention

Expose observations through existing checkpoints/events when both reviews rely on weak proof, assertions assume an unapproved contract, repeated fixes lack new evidence, or the candidate drifts. Do not claim to have awakened Supervisor without delivery evidence or create another detector/schedule.

On a Supervisor question, Lead verifies the observation neutrally, considering a real defect, another cause or no error. Record whether to keep the disposition, check further, reopen or report a blocker, with evidence, owner and next step. Report significant results and Human decisions through the bound Supervisor channel when available. Hold only the dependent work, continue other authorized work and disclose failed delivery via the valid escalation path.
