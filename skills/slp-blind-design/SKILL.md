# SLP Blind Design

Produce a reasoned design decision while protecting the first round from the Lead's preferred answer and sibling conclusions. This is a personalized SLP derivative, not an exact upstream skill. Metadata documents intent; it does not configure admission or grant authority.

## Choose the method

Use when the design space is open and the consequence of choosing poorly justifies independent exploration. Default to two independent design assignments; use a third only when its distinct coverage justifies the cost and the current lease permits it.

For a clear, readily verifiable task, use ordinary implementation and proportionate review. If the main uncertainty is a missing fact, obtain that fact first. Reviewing one fixed candidate is a different method. A Council explicitly requested or required by policy must follow its own protocol; never substitute this skill to bypass its trigger, seats, receipts, or termination rules.

## Authority and delivery

Lead owns method selection, assignments, convergence and the design decision within its lease. Lead must inspect enough evidence to exercise judgment; its own analysis is not an additional independent Peer result. Peers propose and challenge; they do not accept engineering work. Supervisor verifies attention episodes and advises Lead through the bound channel, without directing Peers in ordinary work.

Before launching, verify actual Lead admission, the applicable trigger, current delegation/resource budget, exact project binding and the required tracker checkpoints. Use only available role-bound Paseo tools and approved profiles. Discover the current tool schema; this skill does not invent an API, profile, role, Council identity or provider fallback.

Every participating Peer needs a fresh, parent-owned, read-only assignment with its exact sources, report destination, lifecycle boundary and required issue grants. Follow the existing Beads policy and authoritative readback; do not use Markdown as a replacement issue tracker. Reuse valid prior authorization rather than asking Human again. If a prerequisite is missing, report the exact missing condition before launching; continue unrelated authorized work.

## Prepare one neutral brief

Separate Human-frozen decisions from tentative preferences and technical hypotheses. Preserve authoritative requirements and their provenance. Hide the Lead's preferred conclusion, not decision-relevant facts. Never silently weaken an explicit Human constraint to manufacture more options.

Use the same core brief for every designer:

```text
Decision question and original intent
Observable outcome and acceptance criteria
Frozen constraints with decision/source references
Direct observations, known limitations and unknowns
Exact authorized repository/sources and stable snapshot
Scope, excluded work, allowed effects and resource/time boundary
Required report, bound destination and stop condition
```

Do not supply a ranked option list, preferred verdict, sibling reports, prior advisory conclusions or a parent conversation containing those conclusions. Designers may discover alternatives beyond the initial framing. When a prior conclusion itself is an authorized object of study, disclose that input and limit the independence claim rather than pretending the round is blind.

Use an immutable source snapshot where available. Otherwise identify the actual source boundary, acknowledge mutability and watch for decision-relevant drift; a hash alone does not preserve a snapshot. Give all designers the same corrections to facts or constraints. If such a correction invalidates completed work, mark the affected round stale before relying on it.

Read [the Peer brief](references/peer-brief.md) when preparing assignments. Send only its participant instructions plus the neutral case brief, not this orchestration skill or Lead's private reasoning.

## Independent first round

Launch the authorized designers without exposing sibling identities, issue narratives or reports that reveal their conclusions. Different providers may diversify reasoning when approved routes support them; provider count does not prove independence and a single provider family does not automatically invalidate the round.

Each Peer independently examines the authorized evidence and returns:

- its proposed design, why it fits the outcome and material alternatives considered;
- assumptions separated from observations, with source references;
- meaningful boundaries, dependencies, failure behavior and trade-offs;
- the strongest counterargument and evidence that would change its recommendation;
- unknowns and the smallest useful discriminating check;
- input contamination, source drift or other limits on independence.

Lead may investigate authorized sources in parallel while keeping its hypothesis private. Wait for terminal notifications for all required designers before reading their design conclusions. Inspect lifecycle or delivery failures when needed, without using partial conclusions to steer unfinished designers. A finishing notification confirms lifecycle, not report quality.

No sibling exchange, Lead solution feedback or implementation occurs during this round. Fresh context is necessary but not proof of isolation: shared files, issue text and inherited material can reveal prior conclusions. Do not claim a hard sandbox from instructions alone.

## Check independence, then converge

Before using the proposals, check actual available activity and report evidence for the correct source boundary, initial inputs, first-round completion and prohibited cross-exposure. Record what was observable and what remains unknown. Preserve each original report and its provenance before sending challenges; later revisions must remain distinguishable.

A missing, compromised or stale report does not count as an independent design. A single valid report can inform an explicitly limited decision within Lead's authority, but is not a completed blind comparison. A replacement or rerun requires the existing topology, budget and authorization to permit it; do not spawn repeatedly until a preferred result appears. If the decision requires independence that cannot be established, hold that decision and report the gap.

Compare proposals by the acceptance criteria. Separate disagreements about facts, assumptions and priorities. Verify decision-changing facts; do not merge incompatible designs merely to retain something from each Peer. Agreement can be correct: check its support without demanding artificial dissent or an option C.

After all first-round reports are stable, Lead may relay selected material arguments for a focused response through the permitted channels. Identify exactly what uncertainty the exchange should resolve. Bound the follow-up to that question and the assignment's budget. If discussion adds no evidence, choose an authorized discriminating check, record an unresolved assumption or escalate the specific decision. Unanimity is not a stopping requirement.

## Attention and handback

Expose meaningful observations through existing checkpoints/events: repeated attempts without new evidence, changing premises or constraints, conflicting evidence, or consensus resting on weak proof. Use the bound reporting path; do not invent a detector, schedule, delivery receipt or claim that Supervisor was awakened without evidence.

On a Supervisor attention question, Lead checks the cited observation against the brief and evidence. Consider a real design problem, another explanation and the possibility of no defect. Record the resulting disposition: keep the decision, run a bounded check, reopen the design or report a blocker, with evidence, owner and next step. Advice does not transfer decision authority.

End with a concise decision packet:

```text
Decision, rationale and authority boundary
Source/snapshot and references to original independent reports
Independence evidence and coverage limitations
Material alternatives and reasons for accepting/rejecting them
Remaining dissent, unknowns and reopen conditions
Next action, exact owner, validation and any decision needed from Human
```

Use the existing durable handback and issue lifecycle rules. Design selection is not implementation acceptance or permission to write, commit, publish or deploy. Later implementation needs the proper assignment and review of its actual candidate. End Peer work at the declared boundary; perform lifecycle cleanup only with the authority already granted.

In this personalized setup, route significant results and Human decisions through the bound Supervisor path when available. Only pause work dependent on an unresolved decision; keep independent authorized work moving. If delivery is unavailable, disclose it and use the valid escalation route rather than waiting silently.
